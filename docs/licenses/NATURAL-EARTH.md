# Natural Earth land geometry

Made with Natural Earth. The 1:110 million land geometry is public domain.

- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Upstream source blob: `04811d72fff2701ec67587e30ad8942675b511e3`
- Terms: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/LICENSE.md
- Maintainers: Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso, Tom Patterson and Natural Earth contributors.
- Local derivative: `sim/earth-land.mjs`, with feature metadata removed and source coordinate rings retained.

The procedural game elevations, cities, vegetation, biomes and scale are not Natural Earth datasets. Coastlines are generalized at 1:110 million and do not support street-level accuracy claims.
